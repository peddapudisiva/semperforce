The Problem: Current Reality

Eight systems. Zero shared truth.

Every district already owns the data. SIS, LMS, behavior, MTSS, coaching logs, walkthroughs, surveys, finance — none of it agrees with the others by the time it reaches a board meeting.

Administrative Load

Leadership time is being spent everywhere except leadership

This isn't a hunch — it's documented across national surveys, research institutes and district spending data.

Administrative Load

30% of a Principal's Time Goes to Admin Duties

According to the National Center for Education Statistics, U.S. principals in 2020–2021 spent an average of 30% of their time solely on internal administrative duties — time not spent on instruction or people.

NCES, 2020–2021Source →
Burnout

60-Hour Weeks, and Most Are Burning Out

Administrators can expect to work about 60 hours a week. RAND reports that over 85% of principals felt burnout and high stress in 2022, with roughly a third of educational staff considering leaving the profession by year's end.

RAND Corporation, 2022Source →
Wasted Spend

Inefficiency Costs Districts Over $1M a Year

The least productive districts spend as much as $350 more per student on administration than average. In a district of 3,000 students, that's over one million dollars a year — money not reaching classrooms.

Center for American ProgressSource →
